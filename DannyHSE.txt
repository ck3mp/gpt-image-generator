Two reference images are attached. The first is a photograph of a man's face. The
second is a photograph of a landslide scene: a muddy bank has collapsed against the
back of a red-brick house, crushing temporary metal site fencing, with torn
branches, debris and waterlogged mud across the ground.

Create a photorealistic photograph of that man at that landslide scene.

Subject: the man from the first reference stands in the foreground in
three-quarter view, hands on hips or holding a clipboard, gravely surveying the
landslide. He is a Health and Safety Executive site assessor: he wears a bright
yellow-orange high-visibility jacket with reflective silver bands, dark work
trousers, muddy work boots and a white hard hat. His face is the highest priority:
he must be clearly recognisable as that specific person — faithfully carry across
his face shape, hairstyle and hair length, eye shape, eyebrows, nose, mouth, facial
hair or clean-shaven look, skin tone and apparent age. His expression is serious
and appraising, brow slightly furrowed, taking in the damage.

Scene: behind and beside him, recreate the landslide from the second reference —
the collapsed muddy slope pressing against the red-brick house, the flattened and
buckled metal fencing panels, fallen branches and churned wet mud in the
foreground. Keep the scene recognisably the same incident as the reference photo.

Style: a completely photorealistic photograph, as if taken on site by a news
photographer or a colleague documenting the inspection. Natural overcast British
daylight matching the reference photo — flat grey sky, soft shadows, wet muddy
ground with subtle reflections. Realistic textures throughout: mud, brick, crumpled
galvanised fencing, and the fabric and reflective bands of the hi-vis jacket.
Shot at roughly eye level a few metres from the subject, with him sharp and in
focus and natural depth of field on the scene behind. Ordinary documentary framing,
true-to-life colours, no stylisation, no illustration, no painting, no cartoon —
it must be indistinguishable from a real photograph.

Do not include any text, captions, signatures, dates or watermarks.
