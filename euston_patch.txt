A patch design in the style of the classic NASA "meatball" insignia, but simplified,
and reading "EUSTON" instead of "NASA". A clean flat vector illustration suitable for
3D printing.

Composition: a solid dark navy blue circular disc. Across the middle of the disc, the
word "EUSTON" in bold white capital letters, in the same rounded retro lettering style
as the NASA logotype, large and clearly legible. A red chevron swoosh sweeps across
the disc behind the lettering, like the red vector wing of the NASA meatball. A single
thin white elliptical orbit ring loops around the disc, crossing in front of and
behind the lettering, with a small solid white dot on the ring representing a
spacecraft. Scattered on the navy disc, at most three or four chunky four-pointed
white stars, large and simple.

Style: flat 2D vector emblem, bold thick outlines, large simple shapes, solid fills
only. No gradients, no shading, no texture, no highlights, no 3D effects, no
embroidery stitch texture. Every colour region must be a crisp solid area with clean
edges, as if cut from sheets of coloured plastic. Less detailed than the real NASA
meatball: fewer stars, thicker lines, simpler shapes.

Palette: strictly limited to exactly three colours — dark navy blue, bright red, and
pure white. No other colours anywhere in the design.

Keep all details large and minimal: no text other than the single word "EUSTON",
no tiny instruments, no fine lines thinner than a bold marker stroke, no small stars,
no signatures, no watermarks, no outer glow, and nothing outside the circular disc.
Plain solid white background behind the disc.
